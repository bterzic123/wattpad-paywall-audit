What this is

We opened Wattpad Premium the way a reader does, then checked it against 78,000+ paywalls.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and every year we publish what the data says about paywalls: State of Paywalls 2026. We pointed it at yours. We opened the Premium paywall, switched between both tabs, tapped every plan, closed it, and read your App Store listing.

What follows is four ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, each carries a number from the report, and each is built into a clickable prototype. The prototype uses your design, not ours. Benchmarks are for all categories in the US, your largest App Store market. The report has no Books category.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours: your blue gradient, your two tabs, your black plan cards and lavender selection, the heart and the star, your CTA. Only the order and a few lines changed. This is not a design pitch.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us. We will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.

The four ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Offer changes first, because the report finds they win more A/B tests than visual changes. Each one links straight to the screen in the prototype.

01

Give a cheaper way in when they close

Close the Premium+ paywall today and you are back on Home. Show one sheet first: Wattpad Premium, 5,99 € a month, with what it keeps and what it drops next to Premium+. It is your own price, so nothing is invented.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026Only 4% of apps show a second paywall on close; it beats the main one in 45% of apps.
See the screen ARPU +10–15%Based on Adapty data
02

Put the trial on the annual plan, and say what it saves

Annual is pre-selected, but Monthly sits above it and the free trial only shows up in the button. Move Annual first, tag it “7 days free · save 26%” (99,54 € against twelve months at 11,28 €), and lead with the monthly price.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026Trial on annual, emphasized: conversion +15–30% in winning A/B tests.
See the screen CR +15–30%Observed in winning A/B tests
03

Show how the 7 days work, with real dates

Today the trial is explained in legal text sitting on top of the plan cards. Replace it with three lines: today, the last day to cancel, and the day and amount they are charged. Keep the legal copy, just below.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 (US)Only 24% of top trial paywalls show a timeline. 39.8% of US trials are cancelled within 24 hours.
See the screen ARPU +12–36%Observed in winning A/B tests
04

Put your 4.7 and Editors’ Choice on the paywall

706K ratings at 4.7, and an App Store Editors’ Choice. Neither appears anywhere on the screen where people decide. One line under the headline is enough.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026Only 27% of top paywalls show social proof; in winning tests it lifted conversion 10–30%.

Potential outcome

All four make the same offer easier to say yes to: the plan you want sold first, the trial explained, your proof shown, and a second, cheaper door when someone says no.
